# Bounded campaign stop and structural pivot

Historical research record, retained with its scientific scope. GitHub export is now authorized; see [PUBLICATION.md](PUBLICATION.md). This does not authorize a journal submission or establish novelty.

This report invokes the user's stop rule for searches producing rediscoveries
without a new exploitable contact pattern. It stops escalation of random search;
it does not claim the conjecture solved or the research objective mathematically
exhausted. Baseline validation and the bounded, already justified contact
enumeration remain useful completed work.

## Search space explored

Seed 20260913. 2 400 unseeded eight-parameter tetrahedral contact models,
32 multistart optimizations with Powell and epigraph SLSQP refinement, and
400 attempted five-facet plus 400 attempted six-facet selected-cube contact
models. The recorded final run has 43 421 objective/body assessments and
25 265 distinct rounded vertex records. Many are neighboring iterates in the
same basin, not independent samples or distinct unimodular orbits.

## Negative findings and best candidates

Unseeded random tetrahedra alone reach about 2.802; Powell reaches 3.216;
epigraph refinement reaches 3.41421356237175 numerically. The selected five-
and six-facet families reach about 2.420 and 2.670. These are search outcomes,
not upper bounds for their mathematical classes. Eight rationalized candidates
have exact hollow and complete-width certificates; none exceeds 2+sqrt(2).

The fixed tetrahedral ansatz prescribes a unimodular contact simplex, so its
contact clustering cannot discover the universality of that same simplex.
No new contact-type elimination, global bound, or quantitative stability law
has been established. No million-body expansion is justified by these results.

## Structural bottleneck

ACMS already reduces a global maximizer to finitely many contact hulls plus
the coplanar-square alternative. Fixing contacts still leaves continuous facet
orientations. The coplanar case lacks the full-dimensional contact enclosure.
The complete determinant<=17 contact-tetrahedron list does not optimize even
one remaining continuous family. A second bottleneck is the unenumerated
five–eight-contact hulls within the known volume bound.

## Missing information and reopening criterion

Reopen for a concrete exact inequality excluding a contact orbit, a tractable
semialgebraic chart with a proved finite cover, an independent square-case
reduction, or a candidate that survives exact certification above the baseline.
A larger random sample alone does not meet this criterion.

The useful pivot is finite contact-type elimination with explicit completeness
and dual/branch certificates. NEXT.md specifies work that can begin without
assuming the conjecture. No new mathematical result is advertised; no external
publication or contact has occurred.
