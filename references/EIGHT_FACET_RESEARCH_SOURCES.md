## Inscribed parallelotopes

[Marek Lassak, 2001](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0004972700019195),
*Relationships between widths of a convex body and of an inscribed
parallelotope*, Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society 63, 133–140.
The primary publisher PDF was checked, particularly Theorem 1 and Conjecture 1.
The [institutional abstract](https://www.inf.fu-berlin.de/inst/pubs/tr-b-00-08.abstract.html)
also records the distinction between coordinate widths and axial diameters.
The cube note now records the resulting necessary chord condition.

## Older eight-facet structure

[Basu–Cornuejols–Margot, July 2008 working document](https://www.ams.jhu.edu/~abasu9/papers/3D_lattice-free-sets_proposal.pdf),
*Maximal Lattice-free Convex sets in 3 Dimensions*. The PDF's title-page date
is July 2008; search-engine crawl dates must not be mistaken for publication.

Theorem 1.1 attributes to Howe an infinite two-layer parametrization of empty
eight-vertex polytopes and the extension of smaller empty polytopes to such
eight-vertex sets. Section 2 decomposes an eight-facet maximal hollow body
into intersections of four-plane systems associated with the two contact
layers. Later sections discuss fewer facets under additional contact
nondegeneracy assumptions. The document does not give the present
width-thresholded nine-template list.

This is prior art for the eight-point extension principle and layered facet
description. Therefore the lab must credit those principles as known; only
the explicit finite restriction under its stated width threshold is a
candidate refinement. The document's full proof correctness and eventual
publication history have not been independently audited here. It supplies
context, not a new dependency replacing the existing reduction proof.
